# Growth plan

New or stalled affiliate accounts. Goal: a 30-day plan that tests fast, then concentrates on what earns.

## Steps

1. Collect: platform(s), current followers and average views, niche ideas, time available per day, budget for samples/props, on-camera or faceless, income goal.
2. **Niche**: one niche, three sub-categories. Score niches on: product supply with decent commission, demo-ability, creator's genuine knowledge, mega-sale relevance. Recommend one and say why.
3. **Account setup**: name that signals the niche, bio with a promise ("ของใช้ในบ้านราคาไม่เกิน 300 ที่ใช้จริง"), profile photo, Showcase/link hub ready, affiliate eligibility steps for the platform (`reference/platforms.md`; thresholds `ตรวจสอบใน dashboard`).
4. **Income math**: run `earn --target <monthly THB>` with the niche's typical price/commission to show the views and orders the goal requires. Adjust the goal if it is unrealistic for the account size.
5. **30-day plan** with the phases below, aligned to the next mega-sale or payday window.
6. Deliver the output contract. Done when every day of week 1 has a concrete piece of content assigned.

## Phases

| Week | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test: 3 formats × 5 products, 1–3 posts/day | find the first product or angle with clicks |
| 2 | Iterate: new hooks on anything with clicks; drop zero-click formats | first orders, first sub_id winners |
| 3 | Concentrate: 70% of posts on the winning format/angle; request samples for similar products; first LIVE if followers allow | repeatable sales |
| 4 | Scale + review: mega-sale/payday content push, run `report`, pitch sellers for targeted collaboration or ad authorization | plan for month 2 |

## Output contract

```
Niche recommendation + reasoning
Account setup checklist
Income math (tool output + what it implies)
Week 1 day-by-day table: date | platform | format | product | hook idea | sub_id
Weeks 2–4 rules (what triggers scale/drop)
Weekly review ritual: export → report → 3 actions
```
